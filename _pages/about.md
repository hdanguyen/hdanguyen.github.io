### About Me

**Welcome!**
<p style='text-align: justify;'>
I’m a recent graduate from Fulbright University Vietnam with a B.A. in Economics.
<br>
<br>

Growing up and experiencing social stratification, I’ve always been curious about how and why people end up in very different positions in life. Studying economics has helped me make sense of the world around me—why people make certain choices and how broader systems and policies can influence those decisions.
<br>
<br>

In this sense, my broad research interest lies in public policy with a focus on health, education, and income inequality in developing countries. Using quantitative approaches, I always aim to uncover patterns and potential causal relationships in the data. 
<br>
<br>

This site is a small collection of my modest yet purposeful research journey, containing undergraduate projects, my thesis, and the questions I continue to carry with me.
<br>
<br>

At the heart of it all is a simple motivation: I want to explain economics in a way that feels real and accessible—to my mom and to anyone who’s ever wondered why the world works the way it does.
<br>
<br>

Feel free to get in touch at: <a href="mailto:hanh.nguyendinh.work@gmail.com">hanh.nguyendinh.work@gmail.com</a>.
</p>
